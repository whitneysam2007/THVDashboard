# Verified V2V One-Pager Sample Itinerary

Source: [General V2V One-Pager](https://drive.google.com/file/d/112znoZwaOUAXR76tjwMfFO5ClFc_yH6Q/view?usp=drive_link)

- **Day 1 — Arrive in Guatemala City:** Shuttle to the Barceló Hotel in Guatemala City.
- **Day 2 — Travel to Rio Escondido:** Optional 8:00 a.m. worship services; five-hour private shuttle bus to Rio Escondido.
- **Day 3 — Visit Charmack, arrive in Senahú:** Devotional; visit Charmack Ochoch; arrive in Senahú; hotel check-in; Narú staff appreciation dinner; end-of-day group meeting/sharing.
- **Day 4 — Market Day / Food Deliveries / Medical:** Devotional; medical training; market food purchase/scavenger hunt; clinic support; food deliveries to Narú families; waterfall hike/swim; end-of-day group meeting/sharing.
- **Day 5 — Work Day / Medical / Charmack Dinner:** Devotional; medical training; morning and afternoon Garden Tower projects for Narú families; dinner at Charmack Ochoch; end-of-day meeting/sharing.
- **Day 6 — Sunrise Hike, Cobán Excursion:** Sunrise hike to Senahú Cemetery; devotional; travel to Cobán; LDS Temple/lunch; King Marcos cave exploration; zipline; swimming; paddle boats; lodging; end-of-day meeting/sharing.
- **Day 7 — Travel to Guatemala City:** Devotional; six-hour bus to Guatemala City; stay at Barceló; final group meeting at Barceló.
- **Day 8 — Market Day, Fly Home:** Mercado de Artesanías; shuttle to airport; fly home.
