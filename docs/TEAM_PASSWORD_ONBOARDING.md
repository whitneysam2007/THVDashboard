# Team Password Onboarding

The dashboard uses **individual email-and-password accounts**. There is no shared team password and no email link. Liz, as the owner, creates or resets each teammate’s password from **Team Access**.

## Owner steps

| Step | What Liz does in Team Access |
|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the teammate’s email appears in **Approved access list** and is not marked **Paused**. |
| 2 | In **Create a team account**, enter the teammate’s name, their exact approved email address, and a unique password of at least 14 characters. |
| 3 | Select **Create account**. If an account already exists for that email, this action safely updates its password instead. |
| 4 | Send that individual password to the teammate privately. Do not reuse a password between teammates or post one in a group chat. |

## What to tell a teammate

> Go to the THV Donor Relations Dashboard. Sign in with **your own THV-approved email address** and the password Liz sent you. If the password does not work, contact Liz so she can reset it from Team Access.

## Password resets and access changes

Use **Reset password** next to a teammate in the approved list whenever a new password is needed. Use **Pause** to temporarily block dashboard access while preserving the account, and **Restore** to re-enable it. Team members cannot currently choose or reset their own passwords inside the dashboard; Liz manages those changes from Team Access.
